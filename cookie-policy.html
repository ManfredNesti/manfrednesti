Cookie Policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that websites save on a user's device to store information about their browsing. They can be set by the site being visited ("first party") or by other domains ("third party").

Cookies used by this website

This site is intentionally minimal and does not use any cookies: no technical, analytics, profiling or advertising cookies. For this reason no consent banner is shown — with no cookies to authorise, no choice is required.

Google Fonts

The site uses Google Fonts, loaded from Google's servers. This service does not set cookies, but it does involve sending the visitor's IP address to Google in order to retrieve the fonts (see Google's policy).

Links to external services

The pages contain links to third-party platforms (LinkedIn, GitHub). Those services may set their own cookies only after you click through and are on their sites, under their own policies.

Managing cookies in your browser

You can generally manage or disable cookies from your browser settings: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge.
